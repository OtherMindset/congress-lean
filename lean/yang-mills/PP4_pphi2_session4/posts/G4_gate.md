The proof is one Simon–Lieb step on a square Ω of side l, one Gaussian integration by parts, Dirichlet Green's function bounds by the random-path expansion and the reflection principle, a Schwarz inequality, "standard estimates" for ⟨e^{−2λV_Ω}⟩ in a fixed box, and Jensen for the partition function. They state that clustering of n-point functions then follows by Gaussian inequalities. No cluster expansion, no skeleton inequalities.
