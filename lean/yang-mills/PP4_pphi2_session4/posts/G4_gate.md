2. A finding that changes the route. BFS 1983 asserts its d = 2 steps (Prop. 6.4, Theorem 7.2, Prop. 7.1) as analogues of written d = 3 proofs. But CMP 83 §7, Theorem 7.1, proves the weakly coupled (φ⁴)₂ gap directly and briefly: for Wick-ordered λφ⁴ on δℤ² in a Dirichlet rectangle Λ, there are λ₀ > 0, m > 0 and C, independent of Λ, with the two-point function bounded by C e^{−m|x|} (stated in the limit δ → 0; the proof's constants are independent of δ; for separated points, the two-point function being log-singular at short distance). Wick order is for the mass-1 Green's function; general m₀ follows by rescaling. The proof is a Simon–Lieb step on a square Ω of side l, iterated; two Gaussian integrations by parts ((7.5), (7.7)); Dirichlet Green's function bounds by the random-path expansion and the reflection principle, a Schwarz inequality, "standard estimates" for ⟨e^{−2λV_Ω}⟩ in a fixed box, and Jensen for the partition function. They state that clustering of n-point functions then follows by Gaussian inequalities (the passage to all bounded observables via Newman/FKG is our addition). No cluster expansion, no skeleton inequalities.
