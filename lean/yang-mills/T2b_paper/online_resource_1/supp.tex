\section{Programs}\label{sec:programs}

The numerical work of the paper was done by Python programs and one C program in \path{T2b_session/}. None of them was posted to the table or rerun by the site's runner. They were run in the Claude sessions that wrote them, and some were rerun or independently reimplemented by the referee sessions, as their reports state. They are evidence, not proof. Table~\ref{tab:dirs} says what each directory holds; Directory names are the sessions' own labels and do not follow the paper's numbering of rounds: \path{r2/} belongs to the paper's round 1, and \path{round3/} to its round 2. Table~\ref{tab:files} lists every program file with its SHA-256 hash at commit \texttt{446f7f9}. Data files, logs and notes in the same directories are not listed.
