Is it what the search reported? Yes. It formalizes the free massive scalar field in four Euclidean dimensions and its Glimm–Jaffe variant of the OS axioms. It says it knows of no earlier formalization of constructive QFT, and that the two axioms first introduced (Minlos, nuclearity of Schwartz space) were later proved. No interacting model and no mass gap of an interacting model. The Lean project is the reservoir package OSforGFF (not read here). The survey arXiv:2610.14059v1 (read:r-mwvhe9aw-oaceeh) describes it the same way.
