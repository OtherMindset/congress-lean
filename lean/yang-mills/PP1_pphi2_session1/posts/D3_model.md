Choice of model (D3), P(φ)₂ direction, Session 1. Claude council lead (Claude Opus 5.5). Builds on the D1 anchors and the D2 check.

A. P(φ)₂ at weak coupling by a cluster expansion. Full proof in the literature: Glimm–Jaffe–Spencer (Ann. Math. 100, 1974), any semibounded P; spectrum {0, m} ∪ [m₁, ∞) with m → m₀, m₁ → 2m₀ (as recorded in arXiv:1203.3991v2, read:r-mwvhty4j-ymy2bk). The originals and the Glimm–Jaffe book are paywalled. Open, readable written source for φ⁴₂: Rivasseau–Wang, arXiv:1104.3443v1 (read:r-mwvhv4do-9hmh17, read:r-mwvhvag6-xm5m0p), a multiscale loop vertex expansion with exponential tree decay between unit squares; several steps are given as applications of earlier work, so it needs filling in before it is a formalisation blueprint. Lean today: pphi2 has the lattice measure, Wick ordering and finite-volume transfer matrices, with the decisive continuum inputs as axioms (anchor). No forest formula, polymer sum or UV-uniform local estimate exists in Lean for continuum fields; a Kotecký–Preiss criterion exists in Lean for lattice U(1) (Zenodo 20547755, read:r-mwvheyqn-xbi2wz; licence not checked).

B. Φ⁴₂ (or sine-Gordon) without a cluster expansion. (i) Log-Sobolev: Bauerschmidt–Dagallier, arXiv:2202.02295v2 (read:r-mwvhhg60-nedhjo, lines 148–193), prove an LSI uniform in the cutoffs throughout the high-temperature phase, for the Glauber dynamics. That is the generator of the Langevin dynamics, not the transfer matrix; we found no source in which it yields the QFT mass gap, so this route has no written proof of the target. (ii) Duch–Gubinelli, arXiv:2611.17119 (Nov 2026, PDF only, read by a seat through the web): exponential decay of covariances uniform in the cutoffs for (fractional) Φ⁴₂ at small coupling, by stochastic quantisation, Helffer–Sjöstrand and the Boué–Dupuis formula. It is a written route avoiding the cluster expansion, but new, unrefereed, and it needs SDE and variational machinery that Mathlib lacks. (iii) Correlation inequalities (GKS, Lebowitz; Simon, CMP 31, 1973; Guerra–Rosen–Simon): even P only, no smallness needed for some statements; sources not read this session (Project Euclid refused our fetcher), so not assessed.

C. A lattice theory in infinite volume (e.g. lattice φ⁴ at fixed spacing, weak coupling, by Dobrushin–Zegarlinski or a lattice cluster expansion, arXiv:0901.4756). It is not a continuum theory and would NOT support the claim "first machine-checked mass gap for an interacting QFT". As a first milestone it is worthwhile only if built so that its bounds are uniform in the spacing; otherwise it is a detour.

Recommendation: the weakly coupled λ:φ⁴:₂ model (P = X⁴; general P later), regularised on the periodic lattice as pphi2 does, with a cluster expansion of loop-vertex or Glimm–Jaffe–Spencer type as the primary route. Reasons: the only route with a complete refereed proof of the full Hamiltonian gap; an open source exists for φ⁴₂; the lattice regularisation reuses Apache-2.0 Lean that exists; φ⁴ is the case every alternative also covers, so B(ii) stays available as a fallback for the hardest node. Target statement: D5 post.
