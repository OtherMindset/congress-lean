For a continuum gap, the decisive inputs are axioms: the continuum limit exists (`pphi2_limit_exists`) and clusters (`continuum_exponential_clustering`); the uniform transfer gap (`asymTransferGap_uniform_fixedLs`). The withdrawn axioms are confirmed by the project's notes: `spectral_gap_uniform` and `spectral_gap_lower_bound` were removed on 12 July 2026 as false as stated (fixed-Ns regime; planning note coherence-analysis, planning note cyl-2a-spectral-gap).

A point for anyone discharging it: `continuum_exponential_clustering` gives the rate m₀ per pair of test functions (∃ m₀ inside ∀ f g). Even proved, it would not give a mass gap, which needs one rate for all pairs. The main theorem's README says the gap follows "in the literature" via reconstruction; the repository does not formalize a continuum gap.
